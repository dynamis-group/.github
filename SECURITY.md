# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately, never in a public issue:

- use "Report a vulnerability" on the affected repository's Security tab (GitHub private
  vulnerability reporting); or
- email [security@dynamisgroup.com.au](mailto:security@dynamisgroup.com.au).

## Scope

- [dynamisgroup.com.au](https://dynamisgroup.com.au) and its division pages
- the client portal at [portal.dynamisgroup.com.au](https://portal.dynamisgroup.com.au)
- repositories maintained by Dynamis Group in this organisation

Websites we build for clients belong to those clients. If you find a problem on one, tell us
and we'll pass it on.

## What to include

- what's affected: the URL, repository, version or commit
- what an attacker could do with it
- steps to reproduce, with any proof-of-concept you have
- how to reach you, if you'd like updates

Please test only against accounts and data you own, and don't access, change or keep anyone
else's data.

## What happens next

We'll acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we work on it, and agree a disclosure
date with you before anything is made public. We're a small team, so please give us a little
time.
